Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

VINJAB Group of Companies ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website, submit an enquiry, request a service, apply for placement, or make a booking with us.

Information We Collect

Depending on the service you use, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name and contact person name
  • Phone number / WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • City, location, and other information you provide in an enquiry
  • Booking, travel, guest, placement, or business requirement details
  • Resume or documents voluntarily submitted through our placement form
  • Any other information voluntarily provided by you

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and service requests.
  • To process bookings, placement applications, and BPO/business enquiries.
  • To send service-related updates and communications through WhatsApp, SMS, email, or phone calls.
  • To improve our website, services, and customer experience.
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

Communications

By voluntarily submitting your contact information through our website or enquiry forms, you agree that VINJAB Group may contact you regarding your enquiry, requested service, booking, or relevant business communication through available channels. You may contact us to request that marketing communications be stopped, subject to applicable law.

Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website or conducting our business, where reasonably necessary and subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, or services.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request:

  • Access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of your information where legally permitted.
  • Withdrawal of consent for marketing communications by contacting us.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve your browsing experience and understand site usage. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling them may affect some website functionality.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
